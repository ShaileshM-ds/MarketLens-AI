2. Explicit exclusions and preservation requirements
-----------------------------------------------------
Do not delete or alter:

- The 466 unmatched product-linked jobs. Their records lack run provenance
  and do not match the application's computed run-derived IDs; ownership is
  ambiguous. Also preserve all 771 _repair_backup_20261101_173817_jobs.
- products (6,342 candidate records), sellers (4,013), and product_summaries
  (6,342). They lack per-run ownership markers and have possible replacement,
  API, and retained-analysis dependencies. Their lack of outside observation
  references in one snapshot does not establish exclusive ownership.
- analyses (2,572 at the Phase 4B snapshot). They have no run_id; some are
  synthetic=false. They are shared/product-linked references and are not
  included.
- quarantine (123 rejected source rows). The report proves these belong to
  old source runs, but expressly says to preserve them unless separately
  authorized. This baseline plan excludes them.
- All _repair_backup_* collections, including 1,085 backup observations,
  771 backup products, and 771 backup jobs. Preserve their rollback purpose.
- cleanup_candidates_120_20261103, even though empty.
- marketlens_demo, marketlens_live, users/authentication, settings,
  sessions, limits, watchlists, notifications, unrelated records, and any
  record with uncertain ownership.

The report's watchlist/notification reference counts were zero, but that is
not authorization to modify those collections. No product, seller, summary,
analysis, quarantine, backup, or unrelated-data filter is part of the
proposed primary scope.

3. Recommended sequence and dependency checks
---------------------------------------------
For a future, separately approved execution only:

1. Capture a fresh read-only baseline in marketlens_imported. Recount every
   exact filter above, inspect representative matching records, verify no
   contradictory provenance markers, and confirm the database/collection
   names. Abort on any unexpected match or count change not understood.
2. Ensure the analysis worker is quiescent only through a separately
   authorized operational action. Recompute the run-derived job IDs from the
   application formula; verify the exact ID set, mode/kind, and live count.
   Do not include the 466 unmatched jobs or backup jobs.
3. If separately authorized, delete only the verified run-derived jobs first
   to avoid leaving run-scoped queued work that can continue processing.
   Verify the matched count and resulting count before proceeding.
4. If separately authorized, delete the six verified observation groups
   from observations using the exact filters in section 1A-1B. Verify counts
   by group and confirm preserved/unrelated observations remain unchanged.
5. If separately authorized, delete the six verified raw groups using the
   exact filters in section 1C-1D. Recheck that raw provenance still matches;
   do not infer a one-to-one correspondence with observations.
6. Delete the six run documents last, only if separately authorized and
   after confirming no remaining records requiring those run references.
7. Verify post-action counts for all affected groups and explicitly verify
   preserved collections, demo/live databases, indexes, backups, ambiguous
   jobs, and shared references remain intact. Do not run repair, compaction,
   or other maintenance commands as part of this plan.

The ordering is a dependency recommendation, not permission to execute any
step. If revalidation reveals new or ambiguous references, stop and request
review rather than broadening a filter.

4. Storage impact supported by the inventory
--------------------------------------------
The inventory measured approximate BSON payload sizes for eligible
observations: source about 31.5 MB and generated history about 2.62 GB.
Eligible raw records: source about 111.2 MB and generated history about
4.22 GB. These are aggregate BSON payload estimates, not estimates of disk
space that deletion will reclaim.

The imported database had about 3.07 GB allocated to data storage plus
indexes at the inventory snapshot. WiredTiger allocation, indexes, record
overhead, and the inventory's collection-statistics limitations mean these
payload sizes cannot be translated into a reliable freed-space estimate.
Document deletion may not shrink allocated files immediately. The verified
deployment is local Compose MongoDB; Atlas quota was not measured. Therefore
the evidence does not support a promise that cleanup will free a particular
amount or meet a replacement dataset's storage needs.
